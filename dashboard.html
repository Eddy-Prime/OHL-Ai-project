MAE (Mean Absolute Error)

Average absolute error per match (in spectators/tickets). Example: MAE 875 means you're typically ~875 seats off (too high or too low).
RMSE (Root Mean Square Error)
...
Like MAE, but penalises big misses more. Useful for "worst-case" buffer planning (bigger RMSE = more occasional large errors).
R² Score
...
Explained variance (0–1). R² = 0.67 means the model explains ~67% of attendance variation across matches (higher is better).

What Would Improve It
  • 📅

    More Data — One More Season

    More data is the honest answer. One additional season adds roughly 25 home matches and would substantially improve generalisation at the extremes — especially the near sell-outs and dead-atmosphere Play-off 2 fixtures where the model currently struggles most. The dataset of 71 matches simply doesn't contain enough examples at the boundaries to learn reliable patterns there.

  • 🎟️

    Ticket Sales Velocity

    Not just total B2C tickets sold, but how fast sales are moving day-by-day in the two weeks before the match. A slow-selling fixture that suddenly spikes three days out tells a completely different story than one with steady linear sales. This velocity signal is an early warning system that the current static totals can't capture.

  • 🎫

    Season Ticket No-Show Rate Per Match

    A per-game estimate of how many season pass holders will actually scan in. Currently the model uses a single static variable for season pass holders that barely varies match to match (std of 195 on a mean of 4,321). A dynamic no-show rate per fixture — based on timing, opponent, weather — would directly sharpen the scanned vs. sold gap that is central to this prediction task.

  • ⚔️

    Opponent Away Form

    How the visiting team is playing currently, not just their historical draw. A Club Brugge fixture in a title-race season pulls a different crowd than a Club Brugge fixture mid-table in February. Combining the opponent's recent form with their historical draw power creates a more dynamic attractiveness score than the static is_top_opponent flag alone.
